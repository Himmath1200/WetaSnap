How we made scans more accurate

Problem before:
If the scanner misread a letter or number, you could not tell.
The wrong name, email or phone got saved.

What we added:
After each scan, a small box shows the exact text the scanner saw.
You compare it with the filled form and fix anything wrong before saving.

How this makes it accurate:
1. Misreads become visible. You see "63831" vs "6383338084" and fix it.
2. You check before saving, so bad data never enters your leads.
3. If nothing could be read, it tells you to retake with better light, instead of saving a half-empty lead.
4. Only your live scan pops up, so you check at the right moment.

Result:
The scanner itself did not change. Accuracy went up because a person catches the errors at review time.
