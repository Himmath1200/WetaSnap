OCR Popup - What It Does (simple version)

What is new:
After you scan a card, a small box pops up on the screen.
It shows the raw text the scanner actually read.

Why it helps:
You can see what the scanner saw.
If a letter or number is wrong, you can fix it before saving.
So saved leads are more correct.

How it works:
1. You scan a card.
2. The scanner reads the text.
3. The popup shows that text at the bottom-right.
4. You check it, copy it if needed, then close it or it hides by itself after 12 seconds.
5. If nothing could be read, it says: try better light or move closer.

Notes:
- It does not change how scanning works. It only shows the text.
- Background re-scans do not show the popup. Only your live scans do.
