How people get in

Before sign-in

/signin

Two doors: the WeTano Console has its own address, client admins and staff share the main one. The password comes first; admins then get a 6-digit emailed code. An authenticator is never required — only accounts that turn one on in Security are asked for it. Where a person lands afterwards comes from their account, not from the page they signed in on.
